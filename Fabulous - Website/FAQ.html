FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about how Fabulous showcases independent designers, curation, and inquiries.

General

"Fabulous" is a curated fashion discovery and publication platform. We highlight exceptional work, collections, and lookbooks from independent fashion designers and couturiers, bridging the gap between fresh creative talent and fashion enthusiasts.

No, we do not handle direct sales, checkouts, or payment transactions on our platform. Fabulous operates purely as a showcase directory to publish and promote independent designers' work.

Inquiries

Every piece or collection published on Fabulous includes direct contact information, social links, or inquiry buttons for the respective creator. You can reach out directly to the designer to discuss orders, availability, and custom tailoring.

Pricing varies as it is completely managed by individual designers based on fabric choices, bespoke tailoring, and collection tiers. Please contact the designer directly through their provided details for pricing inquiries.

For Designers

We are always looking for fresh, creative runway styles and independent talents! You can submit your lookbooks, portfolio, or official social media handles through our contact section for review by our curation team.

Bring fresh runway styles to your wardrobe with pieces that double as everyday statements and eye-catching looks.
